Cut mailbox storage costs and digital waste for your team

Newsletters nobody reads don't just cost attention — they inflate mailbox storage you pay for, and pile up "ROT" (redundant, outdated, trivial) data. DontMailMe is the open-source way to stop the inflow, in every account, without anything leaving your tenant.

Talk to us How it works

One move, three justifications

It's rare that the cost case, the sustainability case and the compliance case all point the same way.

€

Lower storage cost

Per-mailbox limits in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace mean junk mail pushes teams toward paid add-on storage. Stopping the inflow keeps mailboxes — and your bill — smaller.

🌱

Cut digital waste

Fewer emails stored and sent means a smaller digital footprint — a concrete, measurable line item for green-IT and ESG goals, not a vague pledge.

🔒

Support data minimization

Less ROT data is easier to govern and retain correctly. And because the script runs in each user's own account, no inbox data ever leaves your tenant or reaches a third party.

Who it's for

IT & admins: reduce Microsoft 365 / Exchange and Google Workspace mailbox sizes, cut the volume of low-value automated mail, and avoid paying for extra storage tiers — using an auditable, open-source script rather than another SaaS vendor with access to your mail.

Operations, ESG & sustainability leads: turn "reduce digital waste" from a slide into an action with a measurable footprint reduction, delivered as a quiet default rather than a behaviour-change campaign.

Why open-source & in-tenant matters for teams

Most inbox tools require granting a third party OAuth access to every mailbox — a security and compliance review in itself. DontMailMe doesn't: the script runs inside each user's own Google or Microsoft account using the official RFC 8058 standard. There's no DontMailMe server in the loop, nothing to add to your data-processing register, and the code is fully auditable.

Rolling it out

DontMailMe is free and open source. For a team you can point staff to the Gmail and Outlook guides, or adapt the open script to your environment and deploy it centrally. Want help scoping a rollout, or a tailored version for your tenant? Get in touch.
