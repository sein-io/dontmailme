# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

DontMailMe is a small, friendly open-source project. We want everyone — contributors, users, and maintainers — to have a respectful, harassment-free experience, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, and constructive.

## Our standards

In short:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree about code, not about people.
- **Be welcoming.** Help newcomers; remember everyone was new once.
- **Be respectful** of differing viewpoints, and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- **Keep it on-topic and professional.** Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as the full definition of acceptable and unacceptable behavior and of enforcement guidelines. Please read it — it governs all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainers at **hi@dontmailme.org**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy and safety will be respected.
