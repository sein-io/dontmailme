# Free up Gmail storage — without paying for Google One

Your Google account gives you 15 GB shared across Gmail, Drive and Photos. Newsletters you never open quietly eat it. Here's how to win the space back for free.

## Step 1: Stop the inflow (the part everyone forgets)
Deleting old mail treats the symptom — the junk comes back next week. The real fix is to stop the newsletters at the source. DontMailMe runs a free script inside your own Gmail that auto-unsubscribes you from the lists you never read, so storage stops filling up. Set it up: https://dontmailme.org/gmail

## Step 2: Clear what's already there
In the Gmail search box, run these one at a time, select all, and delete:
- `category:promotions older_than:1y` — a year of promotional clutter
- `larger:10M` — big attachments hogging gigabytes
- `from:(noreply) older_than:6m` — automated mail you'll never re-read

Then **empty your Trash and Spam**. Deleted mail sits in Trash for 30 days and still counts against your quota — only permanently deleted mail frees space (per Google).

## Honest note
Unsubscribing prevents future storage growth; the bulk-delete reclaims what you've already used. Do both once and you'll likely never see "storage full" again — without paying Google a monthly fee.

## FAQ
**Will unsubscribing free up my Google storage?** It stops new newsletters filling your 15 GB (prevents future growth). To reclaim used space, also delete old mail and empty Trash/Spam.

**How do I free up Gmail storage for free?** Auto-unsubscribe to stop the junk, then delete `category:promotions older_than:1y` / `larger:10M` and empty Trash and Spam. No Google One needed.
